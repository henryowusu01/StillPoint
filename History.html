About StillPoint

StillPoint is a simple mental wellness platform created to encourage people to pause, reflect, and check in with themselves every day. We believe that mental health deserves the same attention as physical health. By taking a few minutes to understand how you're feeling, you can begin to recognize patterns, celebrate positive days, and seek support during more difficult moments. Our goal is to make self-reflection easy, private, and part of everyday life.

Why we exist?

Mental health is often overlooked, especially in many African communities where conversations about emotional well-being can be difficult. Many people grow up believing they should stay silent about stress, anxiety, or sadness because of fear of judgment or misunderstanding. At StillPoint, we want to help change that by creating a safe and welcoming space where checking in with yourself feels normal, supportive, and free from stigma.

Our Vision

At StillPoint, our work is guided by five core values:
1.Compassion – Every person deserves kindness, understanding, and respect.
2.Privacy – Your personal check-ins remain yours, and your well-being matters.
3.Awareness – Small daily reflections can lead to meaningful personal growth.
4.Support – No one should feel alone in their mental health journey.
5.Hope – Every new day is an opportunity to heal, grow, and move forward.
